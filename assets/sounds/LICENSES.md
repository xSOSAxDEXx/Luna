## Still to source

Nothing: every sound in the brief now has real audio, all synthesised in
code (so they loop seamlessly and carry no third-party rights). The tunes are
original, except the music box's "Twinkle, Twinkle", a traditional melody in
the public domain (our own arrangement).

Any of these can be swapped for a recorded CC0 or royalty-free sound later:
add its row above and point `src/data/sounds.ts` at the new file. Until then
a sound can be marked `placeholder: true` to show a **Placeholder** tag.

## Checklist for each new sound

1. Licence allows commercial use in an app with no attribution requirement
   we can't meet (CC0 preferred). Save a copy or link of the licence page.
2. Loop is seamless: no click, gap, or obvious repeat at the loop point.
3. Level matches the noise loops (soft by default), with no sudden peaks.
4. No sharp or startling moments anywhere in the file.
