# Luna Beatzzz privacy policy

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

**Luna Beatzzz does not collect, send or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Everything you make in the app stays on your device.**

## What stays on your device

Your settings, favorites, saved mixes and recordings are stored only on your phone (or, on the website, in your browser). We never receive them. Deleting the app (or clearing the site’s data in your browser) deletes them.

## Microphone and recordings

Luna Beatzzz uses the microphone only while you record a lullaby or story, and only after you allow it. Recordings are saved on your device and are never uploaded. You can delete a recording at any time.

## Backups

A backup file is made on your device only when you tap “Make a backup”. You choose where it goes, for example Files, your cloud drive or email. We never receive it. It contains your recordings, so keep it somewhere private.

## No internet needed

The app works fully offline. It does not contact any server of ours, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

The website version is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. The website itself sends nothing about you anywhere.

## Children

Luna Beatzzz is made for parents and caregivers to use for their babies. It does not collect any information from or about children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. We will never start collecting personal information without asking you first.

## Contact

Questions? Contact details will be added here before the app is released.
